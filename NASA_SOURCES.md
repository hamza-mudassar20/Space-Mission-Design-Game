# NASA Sources and Scientific Attribution

These sources were checked against NASA-authored material available on **14 November 2026**. They supply *reference values* for three playable orbit options; the game does **not** reproduce full mission physics.

| Mission in CYGNOVA | NASA program / source | Credited factual quantity | How it is used in the game |
|---|---|---|---|
| BLUE WATCH — Earth | NASA Science, **Landsat 9** | Approx. **705 km** near-polar sun-synchronous orbit; approx. **99-minute** orbital period | The `Sun-synchronous / 705 km` orbit appears as an option; its repeatable coverage motivates a high Earth-observation science multiplier. |
| POLAR ECHO — Moon | NASA Goddard / Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) **instrument suite and mission** overview | LRO observation geometry / mapping from **50 km** | The `Polar Mapper / 50 km` option increases detail but lowers modeled power/contact margins. |
| RED HORIZON — Mars | NASA Science, **Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter — Science Instruments**, HiRISE | About **300 km** imaging altitude with very high resolution | The `Mapping Orbit / 300 km` option has high science and constrained operations. |

### Essential scientific honesty
**These are NASA-referenced facts:** Earth reference altitude, lunar reference altitude, Mars reference observation altitude.

**These are fictional teaching inputs, not NASA validated mission data:** all launch vehicle names, costs, lift capacities, spacecraft bus masses and power, telemetry/effectiveness percentages, science payload weights, flight durations and hazards, radio data budgets, orbital multipliers, risk scores, achievements and ranking formulas. They are documented as illustrative and are not suitable for engineering decisions.

The supplied Word document mentions the Resources tab but does not include the resource list. We have **not verified** that the three NASA references above appear in that list.
