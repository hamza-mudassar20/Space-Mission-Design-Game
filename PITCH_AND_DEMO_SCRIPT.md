## Strong judge-facing framing
**The innovation is not a pretend NASA-grade physics simulator. It is an accessible, replayable, end-to-end systems-engineering learning loop** connecting science demand, engineering design, unexpected events, collaboration and explainable post-flight learning.

## Five likely questions
1. "Is this physically accurate?" **No:** it is explicitly an educational trade-off model with NASA-reference orbit facts and clearly labeled fictional performance variables.
2. "What NASA data do you use?" Landsat 9, LRO and MRO mission references are visible in the product; verify the official challenge Resources-tab qualification before submission.
3. "Is multiplayer real-time?" **No:** role-based shared-screen hot-seat approvals; real-time online sync is future work.
4. "Can I play it without a login?" **Yes:** static HTML/CSS/JS runs offline and can be served publicly.
5. "Why will students replay it?" Three destinations, three difficulties, modular payloads, multiple orbits, events with opposing consequences, scores, and saved design comparison.
