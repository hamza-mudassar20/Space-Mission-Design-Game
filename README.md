## 🌌 About the Project

**CYGNOVA — Mission Forge** is an interactive, browser-based educational space mission design game developed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**, under the challenge **Space Mission Design Game**.

Have you ever wondered what it takes to plan a successful space mission?

Building a spacecraft is not simply about choosing a rocket and launching it into space. It requires engineers to make critical decisions about spacecraft design, scientific instruments, mass, power generation, communication systems, orbital selection, mission duration, and budget.

CYGNOVA — Mission Forge transforms these complex engineering challenges into an interactive gaming experience.

Players take on the role of a space mission design team, where every decision has consequences. They must design their spacecraft, select appropriate equipment, manage limited resources, prepare for launch, and respond to unexpected situations during the mission.

The ultimate objective is to complete a successful space mission while balancing scientific objectives, engineering limitations, and available resources.

**Every decision matters. Every system must work together. Every mission tells a story.**

## 🪐 Explore Three Different Missions

Players can choose between three mission destinations, each with its own scientific objectives and engineering requirements.

### 🌍 1. Earth — BLUE WATCH

**Mission Objective:** Climate Monitoring and Earth Observation.

Design a spacecraft capable of monitoring Earth's environment and collecting scientific observations.

Players must consider suitable scientific instruments, orbital selection, power requirements, and communication capabilities.

### 🌕 2. Moon — POLAR ECHO

**Mission Objective:** Lunar Polar Ice Mapping.

Design a lunar exploration mission focused on investigating potential water ice resources near the Moon's polar regions.

Players must consider the challenges of lunar operations, spacecraft configuration, orbital requirements, and scientific data collection.

### 🔴 3. Mars — RED HORIZON

**Mission Objective:** Mineral and Atmospheric Reconnaissance.

Design a spacecraft capable of studying the Martian environment, including mineral characteristics and atmospheric conditions.

Players must manage the additional engineering challenges associated with operating at Mars.

## 🎮 Gameplay Experience

CYGNOVA takes players through a complete mission-design journey.

### 1. Mission Selection

Choose your destination and understand the scientific objectives and constraints of your selected mission.

### 2. Spacecraft Configuration

Build your spacecraft by selecting different systems and scientific instruments.

Configure essential spacecraft subsystems, including:

- Spacecraft bus
- Solar arrays
- Communication systems
- Protective shielding
- Scientific instruments

### 3. Launch Vehicle Selection

Choose an appropriate launch vehicle based on spacecraft mass, mission destination, payload capacity, and available budget.

### 4. Orbital Selection

Explore different orbital options and understand their influence on scientific suitability, sunlight availability, and communication access.

### 5. Mission Preparation

Review your spacecraft configuration, resource availability, and engineering constraints before authorizing launch.

### 6. Launch and Mission Simulation

Launch your spacecraft and experience an eight-phase simulated mission journey.

During the simulation, players encounter unexpected engineering challenges involving spacecraft power and communication systems.

### 7. Mission Results and Engineering Debrief

After completing the mission, players receive a final performance score ranging from 0 to 100.

The results include engineering feedback, constraint violations, resource margins, and recommendations for improving the mission design.

## 👥 Team Collaboration — Mission Council

CYGNOVA includes an optional team-based Mission Council that supports up to four contributors working together on a shared device.

Each role has a different responsibility:

1. **Mission Director:** Manages mission objectives, budget, and final decisions.
2. **Science Lead:** Selects scientific instruments and prioritizes scientific objectives.
3. **Systems Engineer:** Manages spacecraft mass, electrical power, and hardware configuration.
4. **Flight Director:** Reviews orbital compatibility, communication systems, and mission events.

Team members review and approve the spacecraft design before launch.

The team mode uses shared-device, turn-based approvals rather than online multiplayer.

### Engineering Calculations

The simulation evaluates several interconnected engineering parameters:

- Spacecraft dry mass and total launch mass.
- Available payload capacity.
- Total mission cost.
- Electrical power generation and consumption.
- Scientific data generation.
- Communication and data transmission capacity.
- Mission viability and performance scoring.

The game uses simplified educational formulas to demonstrate how engineering decisions influence mission feasibility.

**Important:** NASA-referenced orbital altitudes are distinguished from fictional educational simulation parameters. Equipment specifications, subsystem performance values, cost estimates, scoring coefficients, and other gameplay variables are illustrative rather than validated spacecraft engineering data.

## 💻 Installation and Setup

One of the main advantages of CYGNOVA — Mission Forge is that it does not require a complicated installation process.

### Requirements

- A modern web browser.
- Windows, macOS, or Linux.
- No additional software dependencies.

### Run Locally

**Method 1: Direct Launch**

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Extract the project folder.
3. Locate `index.html`.
4. Double-click the file.
5. The game will open in your default web browser.

**Method 2: Using Git**

```bash
git clone https://github.com/hamza-mudassar20/Space-Mission-Design-Game.git
```

Navigate to the downloaded project folder and open `index.html`.

No Node.js installation, npm packages, backend server, or database is required.

## ⚠️ Scientific Limitations and Disclaimer

CYGNOVA — Mission Forge is an educational prototype designed to introduce players to the fundamentals of space mission engineering.

It is not a professional spacecraft mission planner or flight-certified engineering system.

The simulation uses simplified calculations and illustrative assumptions.

The following advanced systems are outside the current project scope:

- Full orbital propagation.
- Detailed orbital mechanics and trajectory optimization.
- Advanced radio-frequency link-budget calculations.
- Detailed thermal control simulation.
- Real spacecraft propulsion and propellant-burn simulation.
- Formal spacecraft reliability and mission assurance analysis.

For additional details, see:

[`docs/SCIENCE_MODEL_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`](docs/SCIENCE_MODEL_AND_LIMITATIONS.md)
